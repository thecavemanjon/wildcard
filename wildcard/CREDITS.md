# Credits and asset sources (Pokémon Wild Card)

What we use (or plan to), who made it, and the terms we found. Anything
marked **candidate** isn't in the game yet. Ideas only from Daily Red and
Radical Red; none of their files.

## In the game

- **Costumes** from RichardPT's Gen 3-style kits (terms and per-kit credits
  under Candidates below): Serena, Ethan, Lyra, Kris, Lucas, Hilbert, Calem.
  Converted to GBA format by us (halved from 2x, palettes cut to 15 colours).
- **Grookey front sprite** (normal and shiny) by lennybitao, "Grookey sprite
  GBA and NDS" (DeviantArt 830957547, 2020). Terms, verbatim: "feel FREE to
  use, just give me some credit and let me know where and how you used it."
  Converted by `wildcard/art/sprites/build_grookey.py` (the sheet's sitting
  frame, mirrored; the battle back is still the expansion's).
- **Greninja overworld** by Jhony-Rex, "Greninja: sprite" (DeviantArt
  559969095, 2015). Converted by `wildcard/art/sprites/build_greninja_ow.py` (down, right
  mirrored for left, up; 28 colours merged to 15; shiny from the old palette).
- **Greninja front** by scorpios_1, "Greninja for @bwohard8, :P" (DinoPixel,
  13 Apr 2024). Converted by `wildcard/art/sprites/build_greninja_front.py`
  (80x80 drawing, top row trimmed to 64x64; the old back sprite is remapped
  onto its palette).
- **pokeemerald-expansion** by RHH (Rom Hacking Hideout); built on pret's
  pokeemerald. RHH asks for credit (their README).

  Trainers Overworld Sprites"). Radical Red credits RichardPT for its Kris,
  Lyra, Alain and Serena overworlds. The Serena post ("Pokemon X and Y Girl
  Protagonist in Gen. 3 Style.", 2019) includes face, **front battle sprite,
  back battle sprite**, walking, running, surfing, diving, bike, acro bike,
  fishing and town map sprites. Terms, verbatim: "Credit us if you using this.
  Credits goes to: RichardPT / Zeak / and others that i forgot the names, lol."

  pokecommunity.com/threads/playable-character-community-project.414973/

  licence in the first post. Credit Slawter666 and iametrine.
